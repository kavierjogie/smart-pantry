# Smart Pantry — brag plan

**What:** A pantry tracker that knows what's expiring and ranks recipes by what you already have, with an AI food assistant that reads your pantry.
**For:** Home cooks who keep throwing out forgotten produce.
**Different:** Recipes are scored by pantry coverage ("9 of 10 ingredients on hand"), and the AI is grounded in your actual pantry + expiry dates.
**Visual hook:** A single pantry card: *Baby spinach — Expires today*.
**Angle / running gag:** Save the spinach. The whole video is a rescue mission for one bag of spinach.
**Tone:** default. Punchy, playful, clean. Brand: white + emerald-600 (#059669), slate text, system UI font, lucide icons.
**Share caption:** "Built Smart Pantry so the spinach stops dying in the back of my fridge."
